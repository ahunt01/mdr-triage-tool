## Visual Identity

### Colors

- Forest Green (primary): #2D5016
- Sage Green (secondary): #7BA05B
- Warm Cream (backgrounds): #FAF6EF
- Deep Earth (body text): #3D2B1F
- Soft Moss (cards/sections): #E8F0E0
- Warm Gold (accents only): #C4973B
- Terracotta (urgency, sparingly): #C06840

### Typography

- Headlines: Playfair Display 700 (Georgia fallback for email)
- Subheadings: Lato 600
- Body: Lato 400 (Arial/Helvetica fallback for email)
- CTAs: Lato 700, uppercase, letter-spacing 1.5px

### Design Rules

- 60% Warm Cream, 25% Greens, 10% Deep Earth, 5% Gold/Terracotta
- Natural, botanical, earth-toned aesthetic — think Kinfolk magazine
- Soft golden hour lighting in imagery
- Never: neon colors, gym imagery, before/after body shots, clinical white

## Brand Personality

- Honest: never overpromise, cite studies, transparent ingredients
- Warm: knowledgeable friend, not lab technician
- Grounded: earth tones, botanical references, nature
- Credible: lead with science, present it accessibly
- Hopeful: no shaming the past, offer evidence-based path forward

## Key Messaging

- "Your body already knows the way."
- "Nature's metabolic intelligence."
- GLP-1 support WITHOUT injections — that's the core hook
- Berberine activates AMPK — the "metabolic master switch"
- $54.99 vs $1,000+/month for prescription GLP-1 injections
- 3,000 years of traditional use + modern clinical validation
- 97% purity vs 50-60% in bargain brands

## Compliance

- FDA disclaimer required on ALL pages/emails: "*These statements have not been evaluated by the FDA. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease."
- Never claim berberine IS GLP-1 — say "supports natural GLP-1 pathways"
- Never promise specific weight loss amounts
- Never use before/after body images in ads
- Google Ads weight loss policies: use "supports metabolic health" not "lose weight fast"
